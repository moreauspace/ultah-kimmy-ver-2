02 · messages

for the girl who reads between the lines

you are not a draft. you were never a draft.

you are the quiet a loud day begs for.

every room learns softness when you walk in.

you carry whole libraries behind your eyes,

and still, you are the most interesting story on the shelf.

the heroines you love would have loved you back

austen would have written you kinder than darcy deserved,

woolf would have left the window open just for your thoughts,

and even the poets would run out of ink before you ran out of light.

you read the world the way others read weather

patiently, beautifully, always looking for what comes next.

you make ordinary tuesday evenings feel like the last page of something good.

you listen the way people wish they were listened to.

you are gentle without being fragile, sharp without being cruel.

so here is the truth, in black and white:

twenty-four is not a number waiting for you

it is a room you already belong in.

03 · creator’s wishes

a wish, written slowly —

kimmy, this is my wish for you: may your shelves overflow and your teacups never sit empty.

may your name be spoken gently in every room it enters, the way good news travels when it is true.

may the hard days pass through you like weather and leave you standing taller.

may you never have to shrink yourself to fit a sentence someone else wrote for you.

may this year hand you chapters you’d underline twice, slow sunday mornings, songs that arrive on time, people who stay without being asked.

and for your family, may your home stay warm the whole year round.

may laughter keep choosing your table, the way light keeps choosing the same window every afternoon.

may health hold every hand in that house and never let go.

may every worry that knocks on your door forget why it came.

may december always find all of you together, in the same soft light, with more to be thankful for than the year before.

may the new year open like a book you actually want to finish slowly, with your whole heart in it.

— from the one who made this for you
